About HyperBuild

HyperBuild is not a workshop or a one-time AI module. It is a continuous AI-integrated lab woven through the PGDM journey at Lexicon MILE.

From the first semester onward, students engage with AI tools, work on industry-driven problems, and build solutions that bridge management thinking with real business execution. AI is not treated as an isolated certification or short-term activity — it is embedded into the fabric of how students learn, decide, and lead.

The result: graduates who don't just understand AI, but know how to apply it where it matters most — in the boardroom, on the factory floor, and across every business function.
