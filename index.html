Program Model

6 Curriculum Pillars

A structured framework designed to develop AI-literate business leaders through progressive, hands-on learning.

01

AI Literacy & Business Thinking

Build foundational AI understanding while developing the ability to connect AI capabilities with real business opportunities and managerial decisions.

02

Functional AI in Marketing, HR, Finance & Operations

Understand how AI can transform core business functions and improve managerial effectiveness across every department.

03

Data-Driven Dashboards & Decision Models

Use data, dashboards, analytics, and AI-assisted decision models to support smarter, evidence-based business decisions.

04

Live Industry AI Projects

Work on real-world business challenges and develop practical AI-enabled solutions with measurable business impact.

05

AI Entrepreneurship & Automation

Explore AI-powered entrepreneurship, business automation, process optimization, and new venture opportunities.

06

Capstone Showcase

Bring together business knowledge, AI capabilities, and real-world execution through a comprehensive final capstone project.
